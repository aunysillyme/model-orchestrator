# Decision log

When choosing an approach, append a concise decision record with evidence. Record the practical reason, not private chain-of-thought.

## <Date: decision title>

- **Did:** <the choice made>.
- **Why:** <the evidence and tradeoff that support it>.
- **Serves:** <the requirement or user outcome it serves>.
- **Rejected:** <alternatives considered and the reason each was set aside>.
- **Evidence:** <source path, command or measured result>.
